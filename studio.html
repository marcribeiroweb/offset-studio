Just wondering?

Frequently
asked things.

A few answers about the studio and how the site is put together.

Is Offset a real business? +

Not as a trading business. Offset is a concept studio built for this web design project.

Can the pages work on mobile? +

Yes. The layout reflows on smaller screens, and the navigation switches to an expandable mobile menu.

Are the project images real photos? +

No. The colourful art, posters and mockups are drawn with CSS—so no photo downloads, JavaScript or external image services are needed.

Does the contact form send messages? +

The demo form can open a visitor's email app. Reliable direct form submissions require a backend or external form service.
